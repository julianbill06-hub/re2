First a few. Then hundreds. Then thousands.

This is said to have happened under Emperor Elagabalus, around 220 AD.
He became emperor of Rome at just fourteen.
And he loved wild parties.
He sat guests on air cushions that suddenly went flat during dinner.
An ancient source says his dining hall had ceilings that could turn over.
Hidden inside: mountains of violets and other flowers.
Some guests are said to have suffocated under them.
But is it really true?
The source is the Historia Augusta. And it's famous for exaggerating.
One thing is certain: Elagabalus ruled for only four years.
He was only eighteen when his own guards turned against him.
