You fall asleep at the gate.
When you wake up, everything is empty.
Every flight cancelled. Except one.
Flight 404. Boarding.
Then an announcement: final call for the final passenger.
Someone stands at the desk. Without a face.
You have two choices.
You walk to the desk.
He slides you a boarding pass. With your name.
Behind you, the lights go out. Row by row.
The jet bridge door opens. Behind it: nothing.
Let's rewind.
You stay seated.
The lights flicker. When they come back, he is one row closer.
Another flicker. Another row.
You close your eyes. And count.
When you open them, it's morning. The gate is full of people.
The board shows no flight 404.
But there's a boarding pass in your pocket.
What would you do? Stay seated or walk to the desk?
